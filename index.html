Costs rise. You start at [Attribute × 10]. Around 40 it is severe, by 80 a crisis, and 108 is the ceiling — past it, that Cost cannot be spent at all. You clear it by taking Wounds, which is how the ending book fills.

Your Bond Technique is Eide. Bonds grant a Technique, Techniques run on Eide, so the thing written on your Bond line pays Stilling.

The listed power is the centre, not the fence. Every level names an energy you can manipulate — counter it, twist it, invent a use. Same difficulty. A genuinely new basic effect may cost more. Ask.

You can combine miracles from the same Attribute into one action. Not across Attributes, and not miracles with Rites.
